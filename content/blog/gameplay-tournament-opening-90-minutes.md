The opening window defines your tournament ceiling.

Most early losses come from weak sequencing, not weak ambition.

## Opening Sequence

1. Verify your baseline state and location.
2. Prioritize sustainable gather routes over random movement.
3. Avoid overextending territory before food flow is stable.
4. Track leaderboard movement after the first scoring interval.

## Common Early Errors

| Error | Why It Hurts | Better Move |
|---|---|---|
| Expanding too fast | Upkeep pressure kills momentum | Expand only after stable inflow |
| Chasing one resource blindly | Inventory imbalance | Rotate based on tile efficiency |
| Ignoring live ranking shifts | Missed adaptation windows | Re-check rank cadence every cycle |

## Recommended Surfaces During a Run

- [`/tournament`](/tournament) for live standings and cycle context.

## 90-Minute Checkpoint

By the end of minute 90, you should have:

- A repeatable gather loop.
- Controlled resource burn.
- A clear plan for the next scoring spike.

If not, pause expansion and re-center on efficiency before pushing riskier plays.
